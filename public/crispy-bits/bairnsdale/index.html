BAIRNSDALE

BAIRNSDALE IS THE COMMERCIAL HEART OF EAST GIPPSLAND AND A CLASSIC REGIONAL VICTORIAN TOWN WITH A STRONG CONNECTION TO THE LAND, RIVERS AND LAKES THAT SURROUND IT. SITTING ON THE MITCHELL RIVER, AROUND 280 KILOMETRES EAST OF MELBOURNE, IT’S A NATURAL GATEWAY TO THE GIPPSLAND LAKES, PAYNESVILLE, LAKES ENTRANCE AND THE WILDER COUNTRY FURTHER EAST. THE TOWN CENTRE MIXES COUNTRY PUBS, CAFES, LOCAL SHOPS AND HISTORIC BUILDINGS, WHILE THE WIDE MEDIAN STRIP ALONG MAIN STREET GIVES BAIRNSDALE ITS DISTINCTIVE COUNTRY-TOWN CHARACTER. LANDMARKS INCLUDE ST MARY’S CHURCH WITH ITS REMARKABLE INTERIOR MURALS, THE MITCHELL RIVER AND THE OLD RAILWAY PRECINCT. BAIRNSDALE SERVICES A HUGE SURROUNDING REGION, SO IT HAS THE BUSY, PRACTICAL FEEL OF A WORKING COUNTRY CENTRE RATHER THAN JUST A TOURIST TOWN. WITH WATER, FARMLAND, FORESTS AND MOUNTAINS ALL WITHIN REACH, IT’S AN IDEAL STARTING POINT FOR EXPLORING EAST GIPPSLAND.
