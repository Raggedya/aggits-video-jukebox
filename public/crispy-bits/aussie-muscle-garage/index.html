AUSSIE MUSCLE GARAGE

MELBOURNE HAS ALWAYS HAD MUSCLE CARS IN ITS BLOOD. FROM TOUGH GT FALCONS, MONAROS, TORANAS AND COMMODORES TO VALIANTS, CHARGERS, AMERICAN V8S, STREET MACHINES AND WILD CUSTOM BUILDS, THE CITY’S CAR CULTURE RUNS DEEP. IT LIVES IN SUBURBAN GARAGES, WORKSHOPS, CLUBS, CRUISE NIGHTS, CAR PARK MEETS, SHOWS, DRAG STRIPS AND WEEKEND RUNS ACROSS VICTORIA. SOME CARS ARE FACTORY-CORRECT SURVIVORS, OTHERS HAVE BEEN RESTORED FROM THE GROUND UP, AND PLENTY HAVE BEEN BUILT WITH ONE SIMPLE RULE — MAKE IT LOUDER, LOWER, FASTER OR TOUGHER. BUT THE REAL STORY IS THE PEOPLE BEHIND THEM: THE OWNERS, BUILDERS, COLLECTORS AND FAMILIES KEEPING AUSTRALIAN MUSCLE CAR CULTURE ALIVE. AUSSIE MUSCLE GARAGE CELEBRATES THE CARS, THE STORIES AND THE MACHINES THAT STILL MAKE PEOPLE STOP, LOOK AND LISTEN. FROM OLD-SCHOOL AUSSIE V8 ICONS TO SERIOUS STREET MACHINES, MELBOURNE’S MUSCLE CAR SCENE IS VERY MUCH ALIVE.
