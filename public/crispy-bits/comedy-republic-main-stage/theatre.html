COMEDY REPUBLIC IS AN INDEPENDENT THEATRE AND BAR IN THE HEART OF MELBOURNE, PRESENTING THE VERY BEST LIVE COMEDY ALL YEAR ROUND. OWNED AND RUN BY COMEDIANS, FOR COMEDIANS AND THE PEOPLE WHO LOVE THEM! • ENJOY SIGNATURE LINEUP SHOWS MAIN STAGE AND GOOD COMPANY EVERY THURSDAY, FRIDAY AND SATURDAY, ALONGSIDE STAND-UP, SKETCH, IMPROVISATION, LIVE PODCASTS, STORYTELLING, CABARET, NEW MATERIAL NIGHTS AND MORE. • ESTABLISHED IN MARCH 2020 BY KYRAN NICHOLSON, RHYS NICHOLSON AND ALEX DYSON, COMEDY REPUBLIC TRANSFORMED A FORMER INTERNET GAMING CAFE INTO A PURPOSE-BUILT COMEDY THEATRE.
