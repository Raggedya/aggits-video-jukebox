BOX HILL IS ONE OF MELBOURNE’S MOST DISTINCTIVE AND FAST-EVOLVING SUBURBAN CENTRES, LOCATED AROUND 14 KILOMETRES EAST OF THE CBD. ONCE A QUIET VILLAGE SURROUNDED BY ORCHARDS AND FARMLAND, TODAY IT’S A BUZZING MIX OF OLD MELBOURNE, MODERN HIGH-RISE LIVING AND ONE OF THE CITY’S GREAT ASIAN FOOD PRECINCTS. WHITEHORSE ROAD AND BOX HILL CENTRAL FORM THE HEART OF THE SUBURB, PACKED WITH RESTAURANTS, NOODLE SHOPS, BAKERIES, GROCERIES AND SMALL BUSINESSES. BOX HILL IS ALSO A MAJOR TRANSPORT HUB, WITH TRAINS, TRAMS AND BUSES CONNECTING IT ACROSS MELBOURNE. BEYOND THE BUSY CENTRE YOU’LL FIND LEAFY RESIDENTIAL STREETS, PARKS, SPORTING CLUBS, BOX HILL GARDENS AND PLENTY OF REMINDERS OF THE AREA’S HISTORY. IT’S BUSY, MULTICULTURAL, SOMETIMES CHAOTIC AND CONSTANTLY CHANGING — A SUBURB THAT HAS GROWN INTO A DESTINATION IN ITS OWN RIGHT.
