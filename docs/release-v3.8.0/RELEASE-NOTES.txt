CRISPY BITS DESKTOP v3.8.0
================================

NEW

- LOVE MY LOCALS is available as a dedicated desktop product tab.
- Operators can enter one to three locations and use FIND LOCALS to build a
  candidate pool of geographically relevant YouTube videos without CSV files.
- The reviewed selection supports up to 50 videos, remove/replace curation,
  ticker copy, one optional EXPLORE URL, preview, publishing, QR generation and
  the existing protected email-delivery pathway.

DISCOVERY QUALITY

- Multi-query Local Texture discovery favours local music, people, news,
  history, community, sport, venues, makers and unusual local stories.
- Generic tourism material is strongly suppressed, property-sales videos are
  excluded, duplicate videos are removed and channel diversity is protected.
- Older YouTube material remains eligible and the final machine order is
  randomised for discovery rather than presented as a ranking.

PUBLIC MACHINE

- Love My Locals uses the approved logo, Midnight palette, location title,
  ticker, reel, lever, video stage and four focused controls: SHARE,
  PLAY VIDEO, EXPLORE and RE-SPIN.
- Experimental EAT, STAY, WHAT'S ON and HOUSE PRICES utilities have been
  removed. Google Places credentials and live restaurant-provider requests are
  not required.

PRESERVED

- Existing Business, Music, Tourism, Banjo, Channel Master and White Label
  projects remain compatible.
- Reel calculations, lever mechanics, landing mathematics, video playback,
  publication, reporting and protected delivery behaviour are unchanged.
- Older Love My Locals development projects containing utility fields load
  safely; obsolete fields are ignored and are not published.

CODE SIGNING

This release contains embedded Windows product and version metadata but is
unsigned because no trusted code-signing certificate is configured.
