CRISPY BITS DESKTOP v3.6.0 — OPERATOR QUICK GUIDE
==================================================

BULK UPLOAD CAMPAIGNS

Open BULK UPLOAD to create a campaign or import a verified UTF-8 campaign CSV.
The desktop does not scrape or invent prospects. Review the candidate details,
run VALIDATE and explicitly approve only the rows you want to build.

BUILD AND REVIEW

BUILD APPROVED creates ordinary Channel Master projects for approved rows, using
the existing YouTube service and the 50-video Channel Master limit. Inspect each
machine in the Library and use the campaign review controls before approving it
for publication. Building a campaign does not publish it.

PUBLISH AND PACKAGE

BULK PUBLISH APPROVED requires the separate publication approval state. After
the selected machines have verified live URLs, GENERATE QR PACKAGE creates the
campaign CSV, manifest, individual QR codes, an internal A4 QR sheet, prospect
cards and ZIP packages. Prospect outreach remains manual.

OPERATOR EMAIL

EMAIL CAMPAIGN TO ME sends one campaign package through the authenticated
Cloudflare Worker. The recipient is fixed by the Worker's secure server-side
OWNER_EMAIL configuration; CSV content cannot redirect the email. RETRY EMAIL
is available after a recoverable delivery failure.

EXISTING PROJECTS

Existing Business, Music, Tourism, Banjo and Channel Master projects remain
compatible. Their UUIDs, slugs, live URLs and QR destinations are unchanged.

VERSION NOTE

Projects and campaigns saved with v3.6.0 should continue to be edited with
v3.6.0 or a later compatible release unless backward editing has specifically
been verified.
