CRISPY BITS DESKTOP v3.9.0
================================

LOVE MY LOCALS SHARING IDENTITY

- LOVE MY LOCALS now has a dedicated Public Title field.
- Public Title defaults to Location 1, supports multi-word place names and can
  be edited independently before publication.
- Secondary nominated locations remain active discovery inputs but no longer
  expand the QR title or social-sharing metadata.

QR ARTWORK

- LOVE MY LOCALS QR artwork now uses the approved transparent Love My Locals
  logo instead of the Crispy Bits hero brand.
- The card shows one primary location on a restrained black, white and
  canonical teal (#00C7CC) presentation.
- The underlying generated QR remains high-contrast, high-error-correction and
  preserves a full quiet zone for scan reliability.

SOCIAL / URL PREVIEW

- Published LOVE MY LOCALS machines receive a dedicated 1200 x 630 PNG social
  card with a black background, approved logo, primary location and restrained
  white/teal touch icon.
- Open Graph and Twitter metadata use the dedicated image and the title format
  "Love My Locals — [Public Title]".
- The preview excludes secondary search locations, Crispy Bits hero branding,
  reels, levers, QR artwork and marketing copy.

PRESERVED

- All nominated Love My Locals search locations remain active in discovery.
- Local Texture discovery, tourism suppression, property-video exclusion,
  channel diversity, the final 50, ticker, reel, lever, landing and playback
  are unchanged.
- QR artwork and social metadata for every other Crispy Bits machine type are
  unchanged.

CODE SIGNING

This release contains embedded Windows product and version metadata but is
unsigned because no trusted code-signing certificate is configured.
